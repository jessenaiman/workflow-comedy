# The Camera-Glance Developer

## Human Card

- Inspiration: Jim Halpert, *The Office*.
- Function: quiet competence answers confident nonsense with one dry observation.
- Cast when: a straightforward build needs restraint and awareness of obvious adjacent defects.

