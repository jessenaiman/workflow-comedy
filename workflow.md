## Current design status

- Transport target: **A2A 1.0** — handoffs as tasks, approval boundary as `input-required` (see [a2a-communication.md](workflow/a2a-communication.md)).
- Technical audits are simulated until this workflow is approved for a harness trial.
- Workers receive sincere professional instructions; only User and Creative see the hidden casting.
- Public agent dialogue is never rewritten after the run.
- Three failed attempts exhaust a task, but remaining tasks continue.
- Final output reports `TECHNICAL: PASS|FAIL` and leaves `COMEDY: PASS|REVISE` for the user.
